# AI Benchmarks Table - November 2026

This table catalogs 365 of the most influential and widely used AI benchmarks, evaluations, and scoring systems spanning roughly the last two decades, with special emphasis on those most active in 2024–2026 and those that moved the field in the past. 

- The collection covers the full spectrum of modern AI evaluation, like:
	- Classic language understanding.
	- Math/coding suites (MMLU, GSM8K, HumanEval, MATH).
	- Agentic and tool-use benchmarks (SWE-bench, AgentBench, ToolBench, WebArena)
	- Multimodal and long-context tests
	- Preference and safety evaluations
	- Data corpora
	- Methodological frameworks that continue to influence research.
- All entries have clickable references to check the original paper or official resource.
